# Introduction to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to the cutting edge of artificial intelligence. This textbook is designed for industry practitioners, developers, and AI enthusiasts who want to bridge the gap between the digital world of AI and the physical world of robotics.

Over the next 13 weeks, you will go from foundational concepts to building and programming a simulated humanoid robot that can understand and execute voice commands.

## What You Will Learn

-   **ROS 2**: The standard operating system for robotics. You will learn its architecture, how to write nodes, and how to manage a complex robot system.
-   **Digital Twins**: How to create and use realistic simulations in Gazebo and Unity to accelerate development and test safely.
-   **NVIDIA Isaac Sim**: How to leverage GPU-accelerated simulation for high-fidelity physics, photorealistic rendering, and massive-scale synthetic data generation for training AI models.
-   **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models**: The frontier of AI, where we use large language models to provide our robots with a "brain" for high-level reasoning and task planning.

## Who This Book Is For

-   Python programmers looking to enter the field of robotics.
-   AI/ML engineers who want to apply their skills to embodied agents.
-   Robotics hobbyists who want to level up their skills with industry-standard tools.

## The Capstone Project

The book culminates in a capstone project where you will integrate everything you've learned to create a simulated humanoid robot that can:
1.  **See** the world through its cameras.
2.  **Understand** your voice commands.
3.  **Reason** about how to complete a task using an LLM.
4.  **Act** on its plan by moving and interacting with its environment.
