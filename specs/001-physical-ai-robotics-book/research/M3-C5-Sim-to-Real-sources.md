# Authoritative Sources for Sim-to-Real Gap and Techniques

This document lists primary sources for understanding the Sim-to-Real gap in robotics and common techniques used to bridge it, as required by Task 1.15.1.

## Primary Sources (Research Papers and Industry Blogs)

1.  **Sim-to-Real Transfer Learning**: A foundational paper discussing domain randomization and adaptive techniques for closing the sim-to-real gap.
    -   URL: [https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.07851](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.07851) (OpenAI paper on learning dexterous manipulation)

2.  **Domain Randomization for Sim2Real Transfer**: A detailed academic overview of domain randomization.
    -   URL: [https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.05314](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.05314)

3.  **Real-Time Sim2Real Robot Learning**: A paper exploring real-time adaptation from simulation to real-world deployment.
    -   URL: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.10186](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.10186)

4.  **NVIDIA's Perspective on Sim2Real**: NVIDIA's blog often covers their strategies for bridging the sim-to-real gap, especially with Isaac Sim.
    -   URL: [https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/bridging-the-sim-to-real-gap-with-nvidia-isaac-sim/](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/bridging-the-sim-to-real-gap-with-nvidia-isaac-sim/)

5.  **Google AI Blog - Sim2Real**: A classic blog post that highlights the challenges and initial successes of sim-to-real.
    -   URL: [https://ai.googleblog.com/2017/11/sim2real-towards-robust-sim-to-real.html](https://ai.googleblog.com/2017/11/sim2real-towards-robust-sim-to-real.html)

## Secondary Sources (Practical Guides)

1.  **Robotics Stack Exchange / Forum Discussions**: Real-world experiences and discussions on practical challenges and solutions in sim-to-real.
    -   Search terms: "sim2real gap," "domain randomization," "system identification," "robot calibration."

2.  **URDF/SDF Parameters**: Understanding how to accurately model robot parameters is crucial for good sim-to-real.
    -   URL: [http://wiki.ros.org/urdf/Tutorials/Adding%20Physical%20and%20Collision%20Properties%20to%20a%20URDF%20Model](http://wiki.ros.org/urdf/Tutorials/Adding%20Physical%20and%20Collision%20Properties%20to%20a%20URDF%20Model)
