# Authoritative Sources for Digital Twin Concepts

This document lists primary and secondary sources for understanding digital twins in robotics, as required by Task 1.6.1.

## Primary Sources (Industry and Academic Leaders)

1.  **NVIDIA's Definition of Digital Twins**: NVIDIA is a leader in simulation and digital twin technology, particularly with its Omniverse platform. Their definition is highly relevant to this book's focus.
    -   URL: [https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/glossary/digital-twin/](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/glossary/digital-twin/)

2.  **Siemens' Perspective on Digital Twins**: Siemens provides a comprehensive industrial perspective on the value and application of digital twins.
    -   URL: [https://www.sw.siemens.com/en-US/digital-transformation/digital-twin/](https://www.sw.siemens.com/en-US/digital-transformation/digital-twin/)

3.  **IBM's Explanation of Digital Twins**: A good overview of the components and business benefits of digital twin technology.
    -   URL: [https://www.ibm.com/topics/what-is-a-digital-twin](https://www.ibm.com/topics/what-is-a-digital-twin)

## Secondary Sources (Robotics-Specific)

1.  **ROS 2 and Digital Twins**: Articles and papers discussing the integration of ROS 2 with digital twin platforms.
    -   (Example Paper) "A ROS2-based Digital Twin for an Industrial Mobile Manipulator": [https://www.researchgate.net/publication/358858392_A_ROS2-based_Digital_Twin_for_an_Industrial_Mobile_Manipulator](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/358858392_A_ROS2-based_Digital_Twin_for_an_Industrial_Mobile_Manipulator)

2.  **Sim-to-Real in Robotics**: The core concept that digital twins enable. This paper from Google AI provides a foundational understanding of the challenges.
    -   URL: [https://ai.googleblog.com/2017/11/sim2real-towards-robust-sim-to-real.html](https://ai.googleblog.com/2017/11/sim2real-towards-robust-sim-to-real.html)

3.  **Gazebo and Unity Documentation**: The official documentation for the simulation platforms themselves are key sources for understanding their capabilities in creating digital representations of robots and environments.
    -   Gazebo: [https://gazebosim.org/](https://gazebosim.org/)
    -   Unity Robotics Hub: [https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Unity-Robotics-Hub](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Unity-Robotics-Hub)
